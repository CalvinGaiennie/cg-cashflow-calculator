    Revenue = (my hours + employee hours) × billable rate + project-based employee revenue + project-based my revenue. Payroll = employee hourly rate × employee hours. Employee project payout = project-based employee revenue × employee share %. Internal labor = internal hours × employee hourly rate. Overhead total = internal labor + Cursor + Hosting + Misc. Taxable profit = revenue − payroll − employee project payout − overhead total (never below $0). Tax reserve = taxable profit × tax rate on profit % — the LLC's profit is taxed whether or not you take it out, so set this aside. Owner distributions are not deducted from taxable profit; they don't change taxes, only how much cash stays in the business. Per employee hour at $75 billable / $30 pay / 35% tax: $75 − $30 payroll = $45 profit; 35% tax on $45 = $15.75; $45 − $15.75 = $29.25 left after tax & payroll (before overhead). Monthly cashflow = revenue − taxes − payroll − employee project payout − owner distributions − overhead total.
